Her story

Twenty years in, and still saying yes.

“

This opportunity arose to rejoin YWAM for a short period of time, and I'm pleased to be able to bring my wealth of experience and knowledge to support the new leadership of the San Francisco location.

Ange spent more than two decades with Youth With A Mission before stepping back. Now she's heading into San Francisco's Tenderloin for a defined season — not to lead from the front, but to help build the infrastructure underneath a new generation of YWAM SF leadership as they grow their reach among the neighborhood's unhoused community.

It's practical, unglamorous work: the kind of scaffolding that lets a ministry serve more people, more sustainably. Ange is bringing her experience to make sure it holds.

The ministry

What YWAM SF does in the Tenderloin

Free showers, hot meals, activities, pop-up church, and discipleship programs work together to create a safe community — a place where unhoused neighbors can find respite, belonging, and practical help.

The Ellis Room

When it rains, the lights stay on. Overnight, the Ellis Room becomes a dry, safe place for neighbors with nowhere else to go.

Meals

Hot meals served by volunteers throughout the year — including a Christmas Day lunch reaching over 200 neighbors.

●

Showers & practical care

Free showers, toiletries, and everyday necessities — dignity in the basics.

●

Pop-up church

Worship and a simple message, right on the sidewalk — meeting people exactly where they are.

●

Discipleship

Ongoing discipleship programs that walk alongside neighbors for the long term, not just the moment.

●

Community & activities

Games, conversation, and shared time that build real belonging in the neighborhood.
