The artist
“No two pieces are alike. Every one is made to be noticed.”

Kindness Grace builds dimension, texture, and life from paper, paint, wire, and cloth — moving between media as naturally as most artists move between moods. Each piece is signed, original, and made to hold a room. Commissions are open.

The work
“The creatures are the most famous thing she makes. They are not the only thing.”

From mythic sea creatures to layered canvases to wire armature forms — the range is the point. Every piece is one of a kind, made to be noticed. Custom commissions welcome. Reach out directly if something catches your eye.
