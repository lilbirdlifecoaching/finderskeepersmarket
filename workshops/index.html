About the workshops
“Every workshop is a little different. That’s the point.”

Kindness Grace runs small, hands-on sessions from her Bay Area studio. Whether it’s paper mache creatures, wire armatures, acrylic painting, or something else entirely — each workshop is shaped around what she’s making right now. The full address is sent after booking.

What to expect
“All materials. All mess. All yours to take home.”

Everything you need is provided. Wear clothes you don’t mind getting paint on. Spots are limited to keep things personal — if something sells out, reach out to kindnessgracee@gmail.com to register interest in the next one.
